Copy trading: what the numbers say

By Jane Writer

Copy trading lets one account mirror another account's trades automatically. Promoters often show a single great week, but a single week says very little about whether the leader has a real, repeatable edge or simply took a large risk that happened to pay off.

When we looked at a sample of leaders, most of the headline gains came from a handful of trades. The median follower did worse than the leader because of delay, slippage and fees, which add up quickly when positions are opened and closed many times a day.

Before copying anyone, look at months of history, not one screenshot, and check how much of the result came from the single best trade.
